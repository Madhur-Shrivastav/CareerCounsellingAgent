Indian Colleges & Universities: An Overview

This guide provides a general overview of some of India's top colleges and universities across five major academic streams: Engineering, Medical, Law, Arts/Humanities, and Commerce/Management. The information includes details on entrance exams, typical rank/cutoff ranges, and approximate annual fees for private institutions.

1. Engineering (B.Tech / CS / Mechanical / Electrical etc.)

Government Institutions:
- IIT Bombay (CSE): Exam: JEE Advanced | Rank/Cutoff (General): Under 500 | Notes: Top IIT, high cutoff
- IIT Delhi (CSE): Exam: JEE Advanced | Rank/Cutoff (General): Under 600 | Notes: Strong placement
- IIT Madras (Engg): Exam: JEE Advanced | Rank/Cutoff (General): Under 1000 | Notes: Known for research
- NIT Trichy: Exam: JEE Main | Rank/Cutoff (General): Under 10,000 | Notes: Top NIT
- IIIT Hyderabad: Exam: JEE Main (Special Round) | Rank/Cutoff (General): Under 2000 | Notes: Strong for CS

Private Institutions:
- BITS Pilani: Exam: BITSAT | Rank/Score Cutoff: 270–380 marks | Annual Fee: ₹6–7 Lakh
- VIT Vellore: Exam: VITEEE | Rank/Score Cutoff: Rank < 10,000 (CSE) | Annual Fee: ₹3.5–4.5 Lakh
- SRM (Chennai): Exam: SRMJEEE | Rank/Score Cutoff: Rank < 20,000 | Annual Fee: ₹3–4 Lakh
- Manipal (MIT): Exam: MET | Rank/Score Cutoff: Rank < 5,000 | Annual Fee: ₹4–5 Lakh
- Amrita University: Exam: AEEE | Rank/Score Cutoff: Rank < 7,000 | Annual Fee: ₹3–3.5 Lakh
